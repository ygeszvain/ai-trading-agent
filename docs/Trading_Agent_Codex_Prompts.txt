Purpose: Build the product described in Robinhood_Local_AI_Trading_Build_Plan.md through small, reviewable milestones. This pack authorizes software development and offline verification only. It does not authorize account linking, live trading, account funding, broker-setting changes, or paid services.

HOW TO USE

1. Use a dedicated repository. Place the existing build plan at:
   docs/Robinhood_Local_AI_Trading_Build_Plan.md
2. Open that repository in Codex. Run Prompt 1, then one numbered prompt at a time.
3. Each prompt asks for implementation, appropriate verification, exact run commands, and an honest handoff. Do not stop at producing another plan.
4. Continue after locally verifiable acceptance criteria pass. When an external dependency is unavailable, record it and continue only independent offline work. Code written against mocks is not verified broker integration.
5. Actual account authentication belongs to a separately operated, owner-controlled runtime. Keep live brokerage credentials and broker-write tools out of Codex and the research model.
6. The coding model and the trading application's LM Studio model are separate choices. The application must operate independently of an interactive Codex session.
7. Use future financial actions only after a separate, explicit owner instruction with concrete scope. Completing any prompt below does not activate those actions.

BUILD SEQUENCE

1 — Repository, environment, and persistent boundaries (M0)
2 — First offline trading slice (M1)
3 — Accounting, execution, and recovery correctness (M1 hardening)
4 — Local LM Studio research integration (M2)
5 — Permitted data and point-in-time storage (M3)
6 — Honest strategy and economic evaluation (M3)
7 — Official Robinhood gateway, with writes disabled (M4)
8 — Full local trading website, scheduling, monitoring, and forward paper mode (M5)
9 — Bounded learning in research (M8 research components brought forward)
10 — Security, fault simulation, and readiness review
11 — Supervised-live readiness code; activation disabled (M6)
12 — Bounded-autonomy readiness code; activation disabled (M7/M8)

WHY THIS ORDER

Numerical strategies, accounting, risk checks, and measurement come before model-driven research. Self-learning begins in a research environment before it gains any live authority. A product that finds no profitable edge can remain useful in research or paper mode.

Use three separate evidence tracks throughout:
- Software: the implementation passes its relevant tests.
- Integration/operations: the real runtime and broker behavior have been observed.
- Investment: prospective, cost-adjusted results support an economic edge.

Never infer that one track passing establishes the others.

PROMPT 3 — HARDEN MONEY, EXECUTION, AND RECOVERY

Follow AGENTS.md, the build plan, and docs/build_status.md. Harden M1 using
meaningful invariant and failure tests. Keep scope focused on financial state.

Objective
Prevent duplicate economic effects and uncontrolled exposure across concurrency,
timeouts, partial fills, cancellation races, and process failures.

Implement
- Durable order states including submission pending, acknowledged, partially
  filled, filled, rejected, cancel pending, canceled, expired, UNKNOWN,
  reconciling, and manual review.
- Persist an intent and attempt before transport submission. Use unique intent
  keys, database constraints, transactional cash/exposure reservations, and a
  single account writer with leases/fencing or an equivalent sound mechanism.
- A timeout after possible acceptance becomes UNKNOWN. Retain reservations,
  reconcile, and block conflicting orders. Never retry a mutation blindly.
- Apply duplicate fill/event IDs once. Correct prior events through auditable
  adjustments; do not rewrite financial history.
- Cancellation requests do not release unconfirmed reservations. Preserve
  partial fills and handle a fill racing with cancellation.
- Reconcile holdings, open orders, cash, fees, distributions, and external
  flows. Do not count pending sale proceeds as settled capacity.
- Separate blocking new exposure, canceling entries, and protective exits.
  A pause must not imply liquidation or confirmed cancellation.
- Preserve drawdown history and authorization state across restart/restore.

Acceptance criteria
Fault-injection and concurrency tests demonstrate no duplicate economic effects,
overselling, or spending of reserved cash. Unresolved state fails closed for new
exposure. Verify restart after each consequential state transition and restore
followed by reconciliation. Do not claim broker exactly-once execution from
local uniqueness alone.

Consequences / Boundaries
Use simulator/mocked transport only. Never call real submit, cancel, or account
mutation tools. Preserve risk policy, accounting conventions, unrelated code,
and failed evidence. No credentials or production changes. Record uncertainty,
block dependent paths, and stop after this hardening milestone with evidence.

PROMPT 6 — TEST WHETHER A STRATEGY HAS AN ECONOMIC EDGE

Follow AGENTS.md, the build plan, and docs/build_status.md. Complete the
research/evaluation portion of M3. Do not tune until a result looks attractive.

Objective
Determine whether the simple strategy merits prospective testing after
execution costs, operating costs, risk, and benchmark comparisons.

Implement
- A versioned evaluation protocol registered before selection: hypothesis,
  eligibility, parameter grid, budget, horizons, splits, objective, benchmarks,
  risk constraints, and rejection criteria.
- Chronological walk-forward evaluation, train-only preprocessing, purging of
  overlapping labels, an appropriate embargo, and an untouched final holdout.
- A bounded experiment registry retaining all attempts, failures, dataset
  manifests, configuration/code hashes, and discretionary changes.
- Conservative event-driven fills: signal availability precedes execution;
  close-derived signals cannot assume an earlier/same close fill.
  Model gaps, spread, fees, slippage, latency, partial/unfilled limits, and volume.
  A daily bar touching a limit is not proof of a fill.
- Compare cash, passive, exposure/risk-matched benchmarks, the numerical
  strategy, and an otherwise identical LLM-assisted variant through ablations.
- Report strategy net P&L separately from product net P&L after data, compute,
  energy, and other operating expenses. Separate realized/unrealized P&L,
  distributions, and external flows; do not double-subtract actual fill slippage.
- Returns, drawdown, turnover, exposure, payoff ratio, execution metrics,
  time-aware uncertainty, regime sensitivity, and trial-selection limitations.
- Stress tests at 2–3 times execution costs, alternate plausible parameters,
  delayed data, missing data, gaps, and removal of the best short subperiod.
- A cost/capital break-even view using labeled assumptions rather than promised
  returns. Report both absolute and benchmark-relative performance.
- Store report series, costs, scope, benchmark methods, sample sizes, trial
  history, and limitations for the website's Performance and Learning pages.
  Later UI values must reproduce these backend artifacts rather than recompute
  authoritative returns in browser JavaScript.

Acceptance criteria
A reproducible report can recommend CONTINUE_RESEARCH, PAPER_TEST, or REJECT.
Negative findings are valid. Identify LLM training-memory hindsight: historical
document filtering cannot erase later facts already in model weights. Label
contaminated/uncertain historical LLM results exploratory and prioritize frozen,
prospective tests. Synthetic data cannot establish trading performance.

Consequences / Boundaries
No live actions, automatic capital changes, paid data, hidden trial deletion,
holdout reuse, objective changes after results, or profitability guarantees.
Preserve evidence and disclose inadequate sample size. If real data is absent,
deliver a tested evaluation pipeline plus a clear missing-data report, not
invented performance. Stop with a reviewable prospective-test protocol.
